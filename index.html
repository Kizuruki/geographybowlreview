🧭 Geography Bowl Guide

Five balanced specialties

    Every region uses the same five topic filters: cities and landmarks; countries and cultures; water; landforms and geology; and climate, biomes, and resources.

    High-yield baseline for everyone

    Learn country locations, U.S. states, major capitals, rivers, seas, straits, mountain ranges, islands, and basic geographic terminology before going deep into a specialty.

    Best study split

    Before prelims: about 70% common-core and compendium practice, 30% specialty. After roles are assigned: shift toward 60–70% specialty while maintaining broad review.

    Game rules

    • Every question is worth one point and there is one round.
    • Full Game and AI Challenge end after 20 minutes.
    • A wrong first attempt automatically passes to the other team after a short throwout window.
    • Each team has two throwouts. Press Throw Out before answering or during the short window after a miss.
    • A thrown-out question is replaced for the same team and cannot be stolen.

    Report Question

    Flag this question if it doesn't belong in a geography competition (e.g. it's really testing history, pop culture, etc). An admin will review it before it's ever removed.
