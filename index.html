Game rules

Simple Geography Bowl format

  • Every question is worth one point. There is only one quarter.
  • Full Game and AI Challenge last 20 minutes; the highest score at time wins.
  • Teams alternate who receives the first attempt. A miss can be offered to the other team as a steal.
  • Each team has two throwouts. A team may throw out its question before answering or immediately after missing, before a steal begins.
  • A thrown-out question is removed from that play and replaced with a new question for the same team.
